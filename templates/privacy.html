NEXORA Privacy-First Architecture

Digital behavior analysis completely decoupled from personal identifiers or typed characters

NEXORA is engineered under the principle of mathematical abstraction: human-computer interaction is modeled solely as a signal of discrete events, velocities, and focus durations. Keystroke buffers, text entries, screen buffers, and window title text are never inspected or retained.

DATA COLLECTED

  • ✓ Interaction Counts: Numerical counts of mouse clicks, keystroke pulses, and wheel scrolls.
  • ✓ Session Timing: Start time, end time, active duration, and idle dormancy seconds.
  • ✓ Aggregate Activity Metrics: Mouse travel distance in Euclidean pixels.
  • ✓ Application Usage Duration: Anonymized executable tags and time spent per application.

NOT COLLECTED (NEVER STORED)

  • ✕ Passwords & Credentials: No keyboard strings or key-down character codes.
  • ✕ Typed Characters & Sequences: Zero log of letters, numbers, or keystroke orders.
  • ✕ Messages & Chat Transcripts: Zero access to communication clients or input fields.
  • ✕ Screenshots & Screen Captures: Zero framebuffer capture or visual frame buffers.
  • ✕ Clipboard & Personal Files: Zero access to copy-paste buffers, disk documents, or media.
  • ✕ Browser URL Contents: Zero URL tracking or inspection of visited web page DOMs.

Data Storage Architecture

Telemetry is saved in an unshared local SQLite database file: nexora.db. No external telemetry relays or remote telemetry clouds are invoked.
